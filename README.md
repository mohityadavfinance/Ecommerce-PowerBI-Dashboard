# E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze the sales performance and profitability of an E-Commerce business. This project provides a comprehensive view of key financial metrics, sales trends over time, and the effectiveness of different sales channels, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Monitor overall financial health via Net Sales, Profit, and Margin.
- Analyze sales performance across different time periods (Year, Quarter, Month, Day).
- Evaluate the contribution of various sales channels (Mobile App, Website, Marketplace, Social Media).
- Track product costs and average customer ratings.

## 📊 Key Metrics (At a Glance)
Based on the current dashboard snapshot:
- **Total Net Sales:** $177.13M
- **Total Profit:** $59.76M
- **Profit Margin:** 34%
- **Total Product Cost:** $97.91M
- **Average Customer Rating:** 3.68 / 5.0

## 🖼️ Dashboard Features
- **Executive KPI Cards:** Instant visibility into the highest-level business health metrics.
- **Sales & Profit Trend Analysis:** A combination chart (bar & line) tracking `Total Gross Sales` and `Profit Margin %` across granular time periods (Year, Quarter, Month, Day).
- **Channel Performance Breakdown:** A column chart comparing `Total Net Sales` across acquisition channels: *Mobile App, Website, Marketplace, and Social Media*.
- **Interactive Slicers:** Top-level filters for `customer_segment` and `sales_channel` to drill down into specific market segments.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
- **Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- **Data Modeling:** Star schema design (implied by the multi-dimensional analysis).
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Created measures for *Profit Margin %*, *Total Net Sales*, and aggregations.
- **Data Visualization:** Best practices in chart selection, color contrast, and layout design for readability.
